Acoustic Signal Processing

How RMS Voltage is Calculated on ESP32

The MAX4466 electret microphone amplifier outputs an analog voltage centered at approximately 1.65 V DC bias (half of the 3.3 V rail). The ESP32 samples pin GPIO 34 (ADC1_CH6) across a 50 ms sample window (equivalent to a 20 Hz fundamental period).

RMS = √ ( Σ (Vraw - 1.65V)² / N ) Estimated dB ≈ 20 × log10(RMS / 0.001V)
